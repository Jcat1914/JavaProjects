Activity 2: Reading

Read the text about Nandaime and answer the following questions

The name Nandaime is a linguistic term of Chorotega origin, composed of two lexicons, "Nanda" which means brook, and "Ime" which indicates abundance. together, it stands for "Place of abundant brooks."

This meaning makes sense because Nandaime is one of the places in Nicaragua with the largest water reservoir, it has 20 water mirrors that make up the lagoon complex called Lagunetas de Mecatepe, some of these lagoons have hot springs of volcanic origin, with temperatures of different degrees. This site is a unique paradise, a place that is located as an area of ​​high potential for ecotourism and alternative health tourism.
